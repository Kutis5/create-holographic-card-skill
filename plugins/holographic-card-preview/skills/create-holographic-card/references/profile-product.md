# Product Profile

Use for a product, packaging, object, or collectible whose form must remain faithful. Preserve silhouette, proportions, material, colourway, visible labels, controls, seams, and identifying details unless `CHANGE` lists them. State the product scale, orientation, support surface, and background relationship when the user has not supplied them.

Use one coherent light setup that reveals the requested material: diffuse light for matte surfaces, controlled highlights for glass or metal, and accurate reflections for glossy objects. Avoid arbitrary redesign, warped packaging, invented logos, duplicate products, or impossible functional details.

For card art, use a portrait 5:7 full-bleed composition with the complete product readable inside the canvas. Do not reserve text zones or put generated text, serials, card borders, UI, or watermark into the raster image.
