# Illustration Profile

Use for drawn, painted, graphic-narrative, or stylised subjects. Specify the medium through visible marks: ink contour, paper grain, brush edge, flat shape, halftone, pencil texture, or controlled vector-like forms. State the shape language, edge treatment, palette role, and light treatment only when they matter.

Turn abstract mood into visible choices such as sparse composition, softened contrast, angular silhouettes, warm paper, or compressed perspective. Do not stack incompatible media or named-brand aesthetics. Keep one dominant medium and one supporting texture.

For card art, preserve a readable primary subject in a full-bleed 5:7 composition; omit raster lettering, logos, frames, UI, and card-game motifs. Do not reserve top or bottom text zones.
