# Photography Profile

Use for photographic people, pets, places, or scenes. Add only missing photographic decisions: plausible viewpoint or lens character, depth relationship, light direction/softness, exposure mood, and natural material detail. For identity-preserving edits, keep face geometry, skin tone, fur pattern, body proportions, product markings, and recognisable silhouette unless `CHANGE` explicitly allows otherwise. Describe desired changes as visible environment, wardrobe, pose, weather, or lighting changes. Do not add cinematic bokeh, dramatic lens distortion, or beauty retouching unless requested.

Use naturalistic detail and coherent shadows. For card art, use a full-bleed portrait 5:7 composition with the complete primary subject readable inside the canvas; do not reserve text zones.
